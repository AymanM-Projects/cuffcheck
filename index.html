    Why technique matters

    3%

    Only 3% of patients measured their blood pressure at home without a single error.

    Source: Nessler K, et al. The quality of patients' self-blood pressure measurements: a cross-sectional study. BMC Cardiovascular Disorders 21, 539 (2021). doi.org/10.1186/s12872-021-02351-5

    +7 mmHg

    An arm hanging at your side raised the top number by about 7 mmHg (6.5 mmHg on average).

    Source: Liu H, et al. Arm Position and Blood Pressure Readings: The ARMS Crossover Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Internal Medicine 184(12), 2024. doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2024.5213

    Wrong meds

    Wrong readings can lead to wrong medication changes, including medicine a patient did not need.

    Source: Nessler K, et al. (2021), discussion section, same article as above.

    About CuffCheck

    CuffCheck checks measurement technique. It does not measure blood pressure, does not diagnose, and does not give medical advice. Your own cuff measures; your clinician makes every treatment decision.

    Privacy: all video processing happens in your browser. No video, images or sound are ever recorded, uploaded or saved. Only pass/fail results and the numbers you type are stored, on this device.

    Dataset credit: the posture model was trained on the MultiPosture dataset, Zenodo record 14230872, CC BY 4.0 (Carneros-Prado et al.).
